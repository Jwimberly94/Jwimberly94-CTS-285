validated requirements:
 The system shall provide feedback after a learner submits an answer.
 The system shall provide a learner with feedback after an answer and allow another attempt after an incorrect answer.


Who recieves the value?:
The learner revieves the value and possibly the teacher or parent.

What capability or outcome is actually needed?:
The outcome needed is for the learner to actually know if he/she got the correct answer and if not is prompted to input another answer
without being confused or wanting to give up.

Why does the capability matter?:
It matters because if the learner doesn't understand whats going on they might not want to continue to use the device. Therefore we lose and they lose.
we lose users and funding they lose out on a tool that can help them actually learn math better and is fun to use.


story:
As a learner, I want to receive feedback after submitting an answer so that I can know whether to continue or try again.

This preserves the validated need, centers the learner, and states useful value without prescribing implementation.
